How it works

Three steps to your certificate

  1. 01Watch and read

    Each lesson gives the key points from the LaserCube manual and X‑Laser’s operator course, most with a short video or pictures.

  2. 02Check yourself

    A few questions after every lesson. Get one wrong? Try another answer.

  3. 03Get your certificate

    Pass the final quiz (8 of 10) and download your certificate as a PDF or an image.

Your progress stays in this browser. There’s no account and nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser data, or switching to another device, starts a course again.

What the certificate means. It shows you completed the course. It is not a licence, a Laser Safety Officer (LSO) qualification or permission to run public laser shows.
