    Read the safety guidelines before your first show

    Ultra MK2 and Pro are Class 4 lasers for professional use. Keep every beam at least 3 m (10 ft) above any floor where people may be present, and set the beam block and connect the safety jumper (US units: the E‑Stop) before you start.

    In the United States an FDA variance is required; a LaserCube bought in the USA comes with it, issued by X-Laser. Many places also require a licence, registration or operation under a trained Laser Safety Officer.
